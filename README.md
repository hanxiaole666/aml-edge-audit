# aml-edge-audit

A reproducible evaluation-protocol audit for cryptocurrency anti-money laundering (AML).

This repository contains the code, per-entity predictions, result files, test logs, and LaTeX
source behind the manuscript submitted to **IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing
(TDSC)**:

> **Cross-Split Edge Auditing for Cryptocurrency AML Evaluation: Degree, Composition, and a
> Pre-Deployment Test**

> **Scope.** This is an evaluation-reliability contribution, not a new detector. The central
> finding is that a protocol change moves reported PR-AUC by different channels on different
> graphs: on the Real-CATS co-occurrence graph at least 90% of the headline gain is a test-node
> *degree* effect (label composition is bounded by 0.012 PR-AUC), whereas on Elliptic, whose
> 234,355 edges never cross a time step, the same operation is composition-driven. We therefore
> deliver a pre-deployment test rather than a transferable formula.

Full title and author: Xiaole Han (sole author), Business School, Qilu Institute of Technology,
Jinan, Shandong 250200, China. ORCID: 0009-0009-9611-2368.

## Pre-deployment test (the portable deliverable)

`docs/predeployment-test.md` is a checkable checklist. Before trusting a cross-split AML number,
measure three quantities on the evaluation graph:

1. number of edges that cross the split;
2. mean number of crossing neighbours received by a test node;
3. same-label excess of those crossing edges against independence.

The **product** of crossing degree and homophily excess decides the dominant channel: a high
crossing degree makes the degree channel dominate (as on Real-CATS, 8.15 neighbours per node),
a low one leaves composition dominant (as under the Elliptic windowed cut, 1.36-1.85). A fourth
question matters for temporal models: **does the split's time index match the index the model
consumes?** At fixed intervention strength TGN-lite's optimism is +0.007 (matching step index)
versus +0.075 (mismatched node-rank): the split sets its sign. Yet TGN stays ~0.06 AP below a
static tree under all three conditions, so the architecture gives a bounded protection. The rule
returns a channel, a sign and that bound, not an AP value.
